Make your cross-stitch patterns, for free

Stitchnook is a complete cross-stitch pattern editor that runs in your browser. No account, no subscription, no locked features: everything is free.

Open the editor

Everything you need to stitch

Drawing

Pencil, eraser, fill, eyedropper, lines, rectangles, ellipses, selection with copy / paste, mirroring and rotation.

Photo or PDF to pattern

Import a picture and turn it into a chart in DMC colors, or bring an existing chart back from a PDF.

Full DMC catalogue

DMC stranded cotton references, searchable by number or name, with a printable symbol for each thread.

Text

Hundreds of fonts turned into stitches, with adjustable weight and spacing.

Progress tracking

Mark the stitches you have done, follow progress per color and isolate the color you are working on.

Print and PDF

Chart split into pages with coordinates, in color or black and white, with a thread key and estimated lengths.

Turn a photo into a cross-stitch pattern

  1. Open the editor and create a project from an image.
  2. Crop it, pick the finished size and the number of colors.
  3. Fine-tune: replace or remove threads, compare with the original.
  4. Confirm, touch up with the pencil if needed, then print or save as PDF.

Why is it free?

Almost every online cross-stitch tool is paid, or heavily limited in its free version. Stitchnook is an independent, open-source project funded only by tips from the people who use it. If it helps you, you can buy me a coffee: it keeps it free for everyone.

☕ Support on Ko-fi

Your data stays with you

No account, no cookies, no ad tracking. Your projects are saved in your browser, on your device. Make a backup from time to time (from the files menu): it is a single file you can keep anywhere. The Text tool loads its fonts from Google Fonts.

Works offline

Install Stitchnook as an app (your browser’s install button, or “Add to Home Screen” on iPhone / iPad) and it works without a connection.
